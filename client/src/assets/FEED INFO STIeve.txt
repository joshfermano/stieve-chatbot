STI College Santa Rosa uses a numerical grading system with corresponding descriptive equivalents. Here's how it works:

Grade | Equivalence | Description
------- | -------- | --------
1.00 | 97.50-100 | Excellent
1.25 | 94.50-97.49 | Very Good
1.50 | 91.50-94.49 | Very Good
1.75 | 88.50-91.49 | Very Good
2.00 | 85.50-88.49 | Satisfactory
2.25 | 82.50-85.49 | Satisfactory
2.50 | 79.50-82.49 | Satisfactory
2.75 | 76.50-79.49 | Fair
3.00 | 74.50-76.49 | Fair
5.00 | 74.49 and below | Failed due to poor performance, absences, or withdrawal without notice
DRP | N/A | Officially Dropped (Dropped with approved dropping slip)
INC | N/A | Incomplete requirements; Applicable only to OJT/practicum courses
P | N/A | Passed (To be used for courses specified as having non-numeric grades)
F | N/A | Failed (To be used for courses specified as having non-numeric grades)

Key Points:
*   A grade of 5.00 indicates failure.
*   INC (Incomplete) is given for unfinished requirements in OJT/Practicum, with a deadline of one year to complete or the grade becomes 5.00.
*   DRP indicates the student officially dropped the course.
*   Non-numeric grades (P/F) are used for specific courses.
